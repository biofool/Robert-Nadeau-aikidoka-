# Robert Nadeau (aikidoka)

A Wikipedia article about Robert Nadeau, an American aikido teacher holding the rank of 8th dan and Shihan (master teacher) in the Aikikai. The repository preserves the Wikipedia article content and is licensed under GPL v3.

## Overview

This repository contains a copy of the Wikipedia article about Robert Nadeau (aikidoka), sourced from `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Nadeau_(aikidoka)`. The article covers his biography, training history with Morihei Ueshiba (the founder of Aikido), teaching lineage, notable collaborators, publications, and seminar activities.

Robert Nadeau (born March 10, 1937) is an American aikido teacher who trained in Japan with Morihei Ueshiba from 1962-1964. He is the founder and head instructor of City Aikido of San Francisco, Aikido of San Jose, and Aikido of Mountain View, CA. He is one of three division heads of the California Aikido Association.
